ℹ️ How it works
  1. Select your document — it never leaves your browser
  2. SHA-256 hash is computed in-browser
  3. Connect your MetaMask wallet
  4. Click Certify on Blockchain
  5. Confirm the transaction in MetaMask
  6. Receive your on-chain certificate

The contract stores only your document hash, wallet address, timestamp and optional metadata. Document contents are never stored on-chain or transmitted.
